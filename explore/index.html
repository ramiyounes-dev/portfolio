Interactive experiments & projects

  1. Quoridor

    A 3D two-player strategy board game. Block your opponent with walls while racing to the other side.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · online3D

  2. Turkish Checkers (Dama)

    A 3D checkers variant with orthogonal movement, mandatory captures, and Kings that move like rooks.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · online3D

  3. Connect 4

    The classic vertical strategy game. Drop discs and be the first to connect four in a row: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · online3D

  4. Tic-Tac-Toe

    Three in a row for two players in an online room. Everyone else watches live and queues up: the winner stays on.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · onlinephone

  5. Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

    Nine boards in one: the square you play decides where your opponent plays next. Win three boards in a row to take the match.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · onlinestrategy

  6. Rock Paper Scissors

    Secret picks, revealed all at once. A quick duel to five, or a free-for-all for up to twelve, with the Lizard Spock variant.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinequick

  7. Prisoner's Dilemma

    Cooperate or betray, in secret. Round-robin pairs, a public history and an optional partner chat where promises get broken.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinesocial

  8. Nim

    Piles of matches, one rule: take as many as you like from a single pile. In misère mode, whoever takes the last match loses.

    2-6 playersvs AI · same device · onlinelogic

  9. Dots and Boxes

    Draw one line at a time. Close the fourth side of a box to claim it and play again; the most boxes wins.

    2-6 playersvs AI · same device · onlinepencil and paper

  10. Reversi

    Trap lines of your opponent and flip them to your colour. Legal moves are shown; the most discs at the end wins.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · onlinestrategy

  11. Memory

    Flip two cards, find the pairs, keep the turn when you match. Picture cards, so it works in any language.

    2-8 playersvs AI · same device · onlinefamily

  12. Hangman

    Take turns guessing letters, or risk the whole word for a bonus. Native word lists in English and French, accents handled for you.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinewords FR/EN

  13. Anagram Race

    Everyone gets the same scrambled word. Tap the tiles or type: the fastest three score. English and French word lists.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinewords FR/EN

  14. Mastermind

    One player hides a colour code; everyone else races to crack it on a private board, guided by black and white pegs.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinelogic

  15. Battleship

    Hide your fleet, then trade shots on a 10×10 grid: miss, hit, sunk. Positions stay on the server, never on your rival's phone.

    2 playersvs AI · same device · onlineclassic

  16. Blackjack

    Everyone against the dealer, and against each other for chips. Hit, stand, double, split; blackjack pays 3 to 2.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinecards

  17. UNO

    Classic rules with private hands: skips, reverses, the Wild Draw Four challenge and a UNO button others can catch you on. First to 500.

    2-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinecards

  18. Bullshit

    Play cards face down and claim the rank, truthfully or not. Anyone can call it out: the liar, or the wrong accuser, takes the pile.

    3-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinebluffing

  19. Codenames

    Two teams, 25 words, one-word clues from the Spymasters, and an Assassin to avoid. Word lists in English and French.

    4-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlineteams

  20. Pictionary

    Draw on your phone while everyone guesses in real time. The faster you guess, the more you score. English and French words.

    3-12 playersvs AI · same device · onlinedrawing
